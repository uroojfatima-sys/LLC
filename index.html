Why People Don't Speak
Honestly at Work

Most workplaces ask for honesty, but power dynamics, fear, and long memories make silence the safer choice.

Why Silence Feels Safer

Fear of Retaliation

Even well-intentioned feedback can quietly follow someone through performance reviews, promotions, and exits.

Power Is Never Neutral

Even in flat organizations, the person who signs your review holds more say over your future than you hold over theirs.

Surveys Aren't Safe

"Anonymous" feedback is rarely anonymous in a team of six — people self-censor the moment they suspect they can be identified.

Honesty Has a Memory

Organizations rarely forget who spoke up, even when they forget what was actually said.

Why Away From Work Is Built for Confidentiality & Psychological Safety

When conversations happen away from workplace oversight, people speak more honestly. Away From Work separates your experience from employer systems and consequences—so you can think clearly, speak freely, and protect your privacy.

Confidential by Design

No Employer Oversight
